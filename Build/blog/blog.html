Complete Guide to Ram Mandir Darshan 2025 — Timings, Tips & VIP Access

📅 July 26, 2025 • ⏱️ 8 min read • By Mahakaal Tours

Planning your first visit to the magnificent Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple in Ayodhya? This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know — from darshan timings and dress code to VIP access and nearby temples worth visiting.

🕐 Ram Mandir Darshan Timings 2025

Aarti / Session Timing
Mangal Aarti6:00 AM
Morning Darshan7:00 AM – 11:30 AM
Madhyan Aarti12:00 PM
Afternoon Darshan2:00 PM – 7:00 PM
Sandhya Aarti7:30 PM

Pro Tip: Arrive before 6:30 AM for the shortest queue. Weekdays are less crowded than weekends and holidays.

👗 Dress Code for Ram Mandir

  • Recommended: Traditional Indian attire — saree, salwar kameez, kurta pajama, dhoti
  • Avoid: Shorts, sleeveless tops, revealing clothing, leather items
  • Footwear: Must be removed before entering temple premises. Free shoe storage is available.
  • Bags: Small bags allowed. Large bags and luggage must be deposited at lockers near the entrance.

🛕 Temples to Visit Near Ram Mandir

Hanuman Garhi

10-minute walk. Visit this 10th-century fortress temple before Ram Mandir darshan — it's customary!

Kanak Bhawan

15-minute walk. The 'Golden Palace' with exquisite Sita-Rama deities and continuous devotional music.

Saryu Ghat & Ram Ki Paidi

20-minute walk. Don't miss the mesmerizing evening Maha Aarti ceremony with hundreds of diyas.

Nageshwarnath Temple

Ancient Shiva temple established by Kush (Lord Rama's son). Focal point for Mahashivaratri.

📋 Essential Tips for Ram Mandir Visit

  1. Mobile phones are not allowed inside the temple. Deposit at lockers.
  2. Carry a valid government ID (Aadhaar, passport, or driving license).
  3. Photography is restricted inside the sanctum sanctorum.
  4. Prasad distribution happens after darshan — don't miss it!
  5. Book accommodation in advance during peak season (Oct-Mar) and festivals.
  6. The best months to visit are October to March for pleasant weather.
