What is the Ramayana Circuit?

The Ramayana Circuit maps the exact route taken by Lord Ram, Goddess Sita, and Lord Lakshman during their 14-year exile (Vanvas). While the full journey spans across India to Sri Lanka, the Uttar Pradesh leg is the most profoundly sacred starting point.

  • signpost
    Ram Van Gaman Marg: The UP Government is actively developing a dedicated 210 km pilgrimage road connecting Ayodhya directly to Chitrakoot, ensuring a smooth and highly symbolic journey for modern devotees.
  • favorite
    Emotional Significance: This route captures a profound emotional arc—beginning with the heavy grief of leaving Ayodhya's kingdom, moving through the devotion of tribal king Nishadraj, and settling into the serene, ascetic forest life of the Chitrakoot hills.

The Circuit Route (4-Day Itinerary)

D1

Ayodhya (The Departure)

The journey begins in the glorious kingdom. Have a peaceful Darshan at the grand Ram Mandir and Hanuman Garhi. Spend the evening soaking in the spiritual ambiance of the Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi.

D2

Shringverpur (The Crossing)

Located near Prayagraj, this is the historic site where Lord Ram crossed the mighty Ganga River. Witness the profound tale of Nishadraj Guha's unwavering hospitality and Kevat's pure devotion at the riverbanks.

D3

Prayagraj (The Ashram)

Take a deeply purifying holy dip at the Triveni Sangam (confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati). Visit Sage Bharadwaj's ancient ashram, where Lord Ram sought guidance before entering the deeper forests.

D4

Chitrakoot (The Forest Life)

The serene forests where Ram, Sita, and Lakshman spent 11.5 years of their exile. Perform the sacred Kamadgiri Parikrama, visit Ramghat on the Mandakini river, and explore the mystical Gupt Godavari caves.

Best Time to Visit

The ideal window for the Ramayana Circuit is between October and March when the North Indian weather is pleasant for extensive temple walks.

warning We highly recommend avoiding the monsoon season (July-September). The hilly terrain and caves in Chitrakoot become slippery and parts of the parikrama path may be difficult to navigate safely.
