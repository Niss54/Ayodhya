Top 10 Places in Detail

1. Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir

Fee: Free Time: 6:30AM–11:30AM, 2PM–7PM Distance: 0 km

The crown jewel of Ayodhya. The spectacular newly inaugurated temple built exactly over Lord Ram's birthplace. The architecture and spiritual aura are breathtaking.

lightbulb Tip: No mobile phones allowed inside.

2. Hanuman Garhi

Fee: Free Time: 4:00AM–10:00PM Distance: 1 km

A massive 10th-century temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman, approached by 76 steps. It resembles a four-sided fort and houses a young Hanuman sitting in his mother's lap.

lightbulb Tip: Visit here FIRST — this is local tradition.

3. Kanak Bhawan

Fee: Free Time: 8AM–11:30AM, 4:30PM–9PM Distance: 1.2 km

Known as the 'Golden Palace', this exquisite temple was gifted to Goddess Sita by Kaikeyi after her marriage. It is famous for its sunset view and intricate idols.

lightbulb Tip: Photography is allowed in the courtyard.

4. Saryu Ghat

Fee: Free Time: 24 Hours Distance: 2.5 km

The highly sacred river mentioned extensively in the Ramayana. Taking a holy dip here is believed to wash away all sins.

lightbulb Tip: Early morning (5 AM) is the most peaceful time for a snan.

5. Ram Ki Paidi

Fee: Free Time: Evening (Aarti) Distance: 2.6 km

A spectacular series of ghats set against beautiful temples. This is the venue for the world-famous Deepotsav and the daily grand evening Aarti.

lightbulb Tip: Arrive 30 mins before Aarti to get a seat on the steps.

6. Dashrath Mahal

Fee: Free Time: 8:00AM–12:00PM, 4PM–10PM Distance: 1 km

Located right in the heart of Ayodhya, this was the original royal palace of King Dashrath where Lord Ram spent his childhood.

lightbulb Tip: Very close to Kanak Bhawan, visit them together.

7. Nageshwarnath Temple

Fee: Free Time: 5:00AM–8:00PM Distance: 2.5 km

An ancient Shiva temple believed to be constructed by Lord Kush (Lord Ram's son). It is the center of huge celebrations during Maha Shivaratri.

lightbulb Tip: Located right next to Ram Ki Paidi.

8. Treta Ke Thakur

Fee: Free Time: Only on Kartik Shukla Ekadashi Distance: 2.2 km

The exact spot where Lord Ram is believed to have performed the Ashvamedha Yagya. The idols here are carved out of single pieces of black sand stone.

lightbulb Tip: It is open to the public only on specific festival days.

9. Choti Chawni

Fee: Free Time: 8:00AM–6:00PM Distance: 1.5 km

A stunning architectural marvel crafted entirely out of white marble. Also known as Valmiki Bhawan, its caves and pristine beauty are unmatched.

lightbulb Tip: A great spot for architectural photography.

10. Mani Parvat

Fee: Free Time: 6:00AM–6:00PM Distance: 3 km

A hillock believed to be a portion of the Sanjeevani Booti mountain dropped by Hanuman. It offers a panoramic aerial view of the entire Ayodhya city.

lightbulb Tip: Visit during monsoon when it becomes lush green.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we cover all Ayodhya sightseeing places in 1 day? expand_more
Yes, if you start early (by 5:00 AM) and book a local taxi or guide, you can comfortably visit the top 10 places in Ayodhya in a single day.
Why must I visit Hanuman Garhi before Ram Mandir? expand_more
According to local tradition, Lord Ram granted Hanuman the blessing that anyone visiting Ayodhya must first seek Hanuman's permission and blessings before having Ram Lalla's darshan.
Are mobile phones allowed inside Ram Mandir? expand_more
No, mobile phones, smartwatches, leather belts, and bags are strictly prohibited inside the Ram Mandir complex. Free locker facilities are available at the entrance.
Is there an entry fee for Ram Mandir or Hanuman Garhi? expand_more
No, entry to all major temples including Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, and Kanak Bhawan is completely free for all devotees.
What time is the Saryu Aarti? expand_more
The Saryu Aarti takes place at Ram Ki Paidi at 6:00 PM during winters and 7:00 PM during summers. Arrive 30 minutes early.
What is the dress code for Ayodhya temples? expand_more
Devotees should wear modest clothing. Avoid shorts, sleeveless tops, and revealing clothes. Traditional Indian wear is highly recommended.
How to arrange transport between these places? expand_more
While walking is an option for some temples, booking a full-day sightseeing taxi with Mahakaal Tours is the most comfortable way, especially for elderly pilgrims.
