The sacred riverbanks where devotees take a holy dip. It comes alive during the mesmerizing evening Maha Aarti with hundreds of glowing diyas creating a mystical atmosphere.

Kanak Bhawan

The 'Golden Palace' gifted to Goddess Sita by Kaikeyi. It is renowned for its exquisite deities of Sita-Rama, ornate gold-detailed architecture, and continuous devotional music.

Nageshwarnath Temple

Established by Kush (Lord Rama's son), this ancient Shiva temple survived through eras and is a focal point of Ayodhya's vibrant Mahashivaratri celebrations.

Guptar Ghat

A deeply revered site on the banks of the Saryu River. It is believed that Lord Rama took Jal Samadhi here to return to his cosmic abode, Vaikuntha.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the must-visit places in Ayodhya?

Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Saryu Ghat, Kanak Bhawan, Nageshwarnath Temple, Guptar Ghat, Mani Parvat.

How to reach Ayodhya?

By air via Maharishi Valmiki International Airport, by train to Ayodhya Junction, or by road from Lucknow (130km).

Is taxi available for Ayodhya sightseeing?

Yes, Mahakaal Tours provides AC taxi for all Ayodhya sightseeing destinations. Call +91-9793434313.

How many days needed to visit all places in Ayodhya?

2-3 days is recommended to visit all major temples, ghats, and heritage sites comfortably.

Is there an entry fee for Ram Mandir?

No, entry to Ram Mandir is free. VIP darshan arrangements can be made through Mahakaal Tours.
