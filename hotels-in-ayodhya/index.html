Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard check-in and check-out time in Ayodhya hotels?

Most hotels near Ram Mandir Ayodhya 2026 follow a standard 12:00 PM check-in and 11:00 AM check-out policy. Early check-in is subject to availability.

How far are these hotels from the Ram Mandir?

We prioritize booking hotels that are within a 1 to 3-kilometer radius of the Ram Mandir, ensuring you can easily walk or take a short e-rickshaw ride to the temple.

Are there pet-friendly hotels in Ayodhya?

Pet-friendly accommodation is quite rare in Ayodhya due to the religious nature of the city. However, please contact us on WhatsApp and we will check specific premium property availability.

Do you offer group bookings or Dharamshala stays?

Yes, for large yatra groups of 20-50 persons, we can arrange bulk bookings in clean Dharamshalas or budget guest houses near the temple at discounted rates.

Are unmarried couples allowed to book hotels?

Many budget and traditional hotels in Ayodhya strictly require a valid marriage certificate. However, several mid-range and luxury properties allow unmarried couples with valid local IDs.

Is breakfast included in the hotel booking?

Breakfast is typically included in Mid-Range and Luxury hotel categories. Budget hotels usually do not include breakfast, but local food stalls are abundantly available outside.
