Day-Wise Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & The Grand Deepotsav

Upon your arrival at Ayodhya Station, our representative will greet you and transfer you to your hotel. After resting, proceed to the Ram Mandir for the divine darshan of Ram Lalla. In the late afternoon, our guide will escort you to Ram Ki Paidi and Naya Ghat. Secure a good viewing spot as the sun sets and the record-breaking lighting of 25 lakh diyas begins. Witness the magnificent Maha Saryu Aarti and the stunning laser show. Return to the hotel for an overnight stay.

Day 2: Heritage Sightseeing & Departure

Wake up early and visit Hanuman Garhi, the fort of Lord Hanuman, for morning blessings. Proceed to Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal to soak in the spiritual heritage of Ayodhya. After completing your sightseeing, our cab will drop you back at the Ayodhya Railway Station for your onward journey, leaving you with memories of a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Deepotsav Ayodhya tour package?

The 1N2D package includes pickup from Ayodhya station, hotel stay near Ram Ki Paidi, Ram Mandir darshan coordination, and a guided evening visit to witness the festival.

Is hotel accommodation included for Deepotsav?

Yes, our ₹3,499 package includes a 1-night stay in a comfortable hotel near the main celebration areas.

When should I book the Deepotsav 2026 package?

Deepotsav is the most crowded time in Ayodhya. We highly recommend booking 4 to 6 weeks in advance to secure hotel availability.

Will I get VIP darshan during Deepotsav?

Official Sugam darshan slots are usually suspended during Deepotsav. However, our local guides will coordinate your visit to ensure a smooth darshan experience.

How do we travel within the city during Deepotsav?

Due to heavy traffic restrictions, four-wheelers are banned near Ram Ki Paidi. We arrange walking tours or e-rickshaw transport for easy access.
