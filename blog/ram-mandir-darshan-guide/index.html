2. How to book Ram Mandir VIP pass

If you prefer to book the free Sugam pass yourself, the Ram Mandir slot booking process requires extreme punctuality as slots vanish within minutes of opening. Here is the exact step-by-step process:

  1. Login early: Visit the official Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Teerth Kshetra portal at least 15 minutes before the midnight slot refresh.
  2. OTP Verification: Keep your mobile phone handy. Enter your number and verify the OTP instantly.
  3. Select Date & Slot: Choose your preferred Ram Lalla darshan timing 2026. Morning slots (7:00 AM) are highly recommended.
  4. Upload ID Proof: You must upload a clear photo of your Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport. The name must match exactly.
  5. Download QR Code: Save the final PDF with the QR code. You MUST print this out; digital copies are often delayed at security scanners.

3. Ram Mandir Aarti Pass Booking

Witnessing the divine Aarti is a transcendent experience. The Ram Mandir aarti pass booking is strictly limited to a few dozen devotees per session due to space constraints inside the sanctum sanctorum.

  • Mangala Aarti (4:30 AM): Requires booking 15-30 days in advance. Supreme spiritual bliss.
  • Shringar Aarti (6:30 AM): Beautiful morning rituals. Moderate availability.
  • Shayan Aarti (7:30 PM): Evening resting rituals. High demand among overnight tourists.

4. Ram Lalla Darshan Timing 2026 & Best Times to Visit

The temple operates in two primary shifts. Knowing the Ram Lalla darshan timing 2026 will help you plan your itinerary:

  • Morning Shift: 6:30 AM to 11:30 AM
  • Afternoon Rest (Bhagwan Vishram): 11:30 AM to 2:00 PM (Temple Closed)
  • Evening Shift: 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM

So, what is the best time for darshan Ayodhya? Our local guides unanimously agree that arriving at the entry gates at 6:00 AM or 3:00 PM yields the fastest darshan and most pleasant weather.

6. Pro Tips for a Hassle-Free Darshan

  • Arrive 45 Mins Early: Always reach the entry gate 45 minutes prior to your booked slot to clear multi-tier security checks.
  • No Mobiles Inside: Electronic devices, smartwatches, and phones are strictly prohibited. Deposit them at the free Pilgrim Facilitation Centre lockers.
  • Dress Code: Wear modest, traditional attire. Sleeveless clothes or shorts are highly discouraged.
  • Hydration: Carry a water bottle, especially if standing in the general queue during summers.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Ram Mandir VIP Darshan Booking 2026 price?

The official Sugam darshan pass is free. However, premium VIP packages via agencies offering cab, hotel, and fast-track assistance range from ₹500 to ₹1,000.

Is Ram Mandir entry without pass allowed?

Yes, Ram Mandir entry without pass is permitted through the general queue, but waiting times can exceed 3-4 hours during weekends.

What is the Sugam vs VIP darshan difference?

Sugam Darshan is a free pre-booked slot that reduces waiting time to 45 mins. VIP Darshan often involves dedicated guides, closest viewing proximity, and priority entry points.

What is the best time for darshan Ayodhya?

The best time for darshan Ayodhya is early morning between 6:30 AM to 8:00 AM, or late evening during the Shringar Aarti.

Are mobile phones allowed inside the Ram Mandir?

No, mobile phones, cameras, and smartwatches are strictly prohibited. You must deposit them at the free locker facility before entering the security check.

What is the dress code for Ram Mandir Ayodhya?

While there is no strict official dress code, devotees are expected to wear modest, traditional clothing. Shorts and sleeveless tops are highly discouraged.

Can I book Ram Mandir Aarti pass offline?

No, Aarti passes (Mangala, Shringar, Shayan) are extremely limited and must be booked online through the official portal well in advance.

Is VIP darshan available during Ram Navami?

Usually, all VIP passes and Sugam darshan slots are suspended during peak festival days like Ram Navami and Deepotsav to accommodate the massive general crowds.
