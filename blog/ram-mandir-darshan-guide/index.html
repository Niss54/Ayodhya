2. How to book Ram Mandir VIP pass

If you prefer to book the free Sugam pass yourself, the Ram Mandir slot booking process requires extreme punctuality as slots vanish within minutes of opening. Here is the exact step-by-step process:

  1. Login early: Visit the official Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Teerth Kshetra portal at least 15 minutes before the midnight slot refresh.
  2. OTP Verification: Keep your mobile phone handy. Enter your number and verify the OTP instantly.
  3. Select Date & Slot: Choose your preferred Ram Lalla darshan timing 2026. Morning slots (7:00 AM) are highly recommended.
  4. Upload ID Proof: You must upload a clear photo of your Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport. The name must match exactly.
  5. Download QR Code: Save the final PDF with the QR code. You MUST print this out; digital copies are often delayed at security scanners.

3. Ram Mandir Aarti Pass Booking

Witnessing the divine Aarti is a transcendent experience. The Ram Mandir aarti pass booking is strictly limited to a few dozen devotees per session due to space constraints inside the sanctum sanctorum.

  • Mangala Aarti (4:30 AM): Requires booking 15-30 days in advance. Supreme spiritual bliss.
  • Shringar Aarti (6:30 AM): Beautiful morning rituals. Moderate availability.
  • Shayan Aarti (7:30 PM): Evening resting rituals. High demand among overnight tourists.

4. Ram Lalla Darshan Timing 2026 & Best Times to Visit

The temple operates in two primary shifts. Knowing the Ram Lalla darshan timing 2026 will help you plan your itinerary:

  • Morning Shift: 6:30 AM to 11:30 AM
  • Afternoon Rest (Bhagwan Vishram): 11:30 AM to 2:00 PM (Temple Closed)
  • Evening Shift: 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM

So, what is the best time for darshan Ayodhya? Our local guides unanimously agree that arriving at the entry gates at 6:00 AM or 3:00 PM yields the fastest darshan and most pleasant weather.
