Ayodhya Taxi Rates 2026: Complete Fare Guide for Local & Outstation Cab

Planning a trip to Ram Mandir? Avoid hidden charges and tourist traps with our comprehensive and transparent guide to the latest cab fare Ayodhya.

calendar_month Updated: August 2026 schedule 6 Min Read

With millions of pilgrims visiting Ayodhya annually, reliable transportation is a top priority. Knowing the standard Ayodhya taxi rates 2026 is essential to ensure you aren't overcharged. Whether you need a local cab for temple darshan or an outstation drop to Varanasi or Lucknow, this guide breaks down every exact cab fare Ayodhya.

1. Standard Per-Km Taxi Rates

If you are booking a cab for a multi-day outstation tour, travel agencies generally charge on a per-kilometer basis. A minimum daily running distance of 250 kilometers is standard across Uttar Pradesh.

  • Hatchback (Swift, WagonR): ₹9 to ₹11 / km
  • Sedan (Swift Dzire, Etios): ₹12 to ₹14 / km
  • SUV (Ertiga, Innova Crysta): ₹16 to ₹18 / km

3. How to Save on Taxi Bookings in Ayodhya

Traveling smartly can save you thousands of rupees. Here are our top tips for reducing your cab fare Ayodhya:

  • Book in Advance: Last-minute bookings at the airport or railway station are often subject to huge premiums.
  • WhatsApp Booking: Booking directly with a local agency via WhatsApp avoids aggregator commissions. You get direct contact with the driver.
  • Package Deals: Instead of booking a cab and hotel separately, opting for a complete Ayodhya Tour Package often results in a 15-20% overall discount.

4. Mahakaal Tours vs OLA/Uber: The Local Advantage

Many tourists attempt to rely on app-based aggregators like Ola or Uber in Ayodhya. However, Ayodhya's local geography features many narrow, restricted lanes leading up to the Ram Mandir. Local agencies like Mahakaal Tours have drivers who know the inner routes, traffic restrictions, and parking zones perfectly. Furthermore, app-based cabs suffer from high cancellation rates during peak hours, whereas pre-booking with a local agency guarantees your ride.

campaign Festival Season Pricing Note

Please note that during major festivals like Deepotsav and Ram Navami, extreme demand and severe traffic diversions cause a surge in prices. Taxi rates during these specific dates may increase by 20% to 30%. We strongly advise booking your cab 4-6 weeks in advance for festival dates.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the cab fare Ayodhya for a full day?

A full day (8 hours / 80 kms) cab fare in Ayodhya starts at ₹1,800 for a Swift Dzire and ₹2,500 for a 6-seater Ertiga.

Is Ola/Uber available in Ayodhya?

While Ola and Uber have limited presence in Ayodhya, finding a cab during peak hours is very difficult. Booking with a local agency ensures a guaranteed ride without last-minute cancellations.

What is the taxi rate from Ayodhya to Varanasi?

The one-way taxi fare from Ayodhya to Varanasi ranges between ₹3,000 for a Sedan to ₹5,000 for an SUV like Innova.

Do taxi rates increase during Deepotsav or Ram Navami?

Yes, due to extreme demand and traffic restrictions, taxi rates may increase by 20-30% during major festivals. Advance booking is highly recommended.

Are parking and toll taxes included in the fare?

Generally, parking, toll taxes, and driver allowances are not included in the base per-km or local package fare and must be paid extra by the customer.
