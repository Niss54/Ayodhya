Ayodhya, the sacred birthplace of Lord Ram, is a city that vibrates with spiritual energy throughout the year. However, the extreme North Indian weather and massive festival crowds mean that timing your trip is crucial. This complete 2026 evergreen guide breaks down Ayodhya's seasons, month-by-month conditions, and exclusive pro-tips you won't find anywhere else.

Season-by-Season Breakdown

ac_unit

Winter (Oct–Mar)

Temperature: 8°C - 25°C

The Ideal Season. The weather is highly comfortable for long walks between temples. Almost all major festivals (Deepotsav, Vivah Panchami) fall in this period.

local_fire_department

Summer (Apr–Jun)

Temperature: 28°C - 43°C

Hot & Dry. Expect intense heat. However, Ram Navami usually occurs in April. Aside from the festival, you'll find fewer crowds and cheaper hotel rates.

water_drop

Monsoon (Jul–Sep)

Temperature: 24°C - 32°C

Peaceful & Scenic. Frequent rains wash the city, offering beautiful views of the swollen Saryu River. There is some minor flooding risk, but the crowds are very low.

Festival Calendar 2026 (Mark Your Dates)

Mar 26 2026

Ram Navami

The birth of Lord Ram. The highlight of 2026 will be the Surya Tilak at exactly 12:00 PM, where sunlight will be mechanically channeled directly onto the forehead of Ram Lalla. Millions gather for this auspicious moment.

Nov 07 2026

Deepotsav (Diwali)

Ayodhya's most spectacular event. In 2026, the administration plans to break world records again by lighting over 25+ lakh diyas along the Saryu River at Ram ki Paidi. The atmosphere is magical.

Nov 24 2026

Kartik Purnima

Devotees arrive in massive numbers for the holy Saryu snan (bath) at dawn, washing away their sins on this highly auspicious full moon day.

lightbulb Exclusive Insider Pro-Tips

Competitors don't tell you this. As locals, here are our top secrets to navigating Ayodhya:

  • check_circle
    Saryu Aarti Timing: The beautiful Saryu Aarti takes place every evening. In Summer, it starts at 7:00 PM, and in Winter at 6:00 PM. Pro-Tip: Arrive at least 30 minutes early to secure a spot on the steps.
  • check_circle
    Ram Mandir Peak Queue: The absolute worst time for Darshan is between 11 AM and 4 PM. Pro-Tip: Enter the complex between 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM or after 5:00 PM for a much faster and peaceful experience.
  • check_circle
    Deepotsav Bookings: Every decent hotel in Ayodhya books out 3 months before Deepotsav. Pro-Tip: If you want to visit on Nov 7, 2026, your bookings must be finalized by August!
  • check_circle
    Hanuman Garhi Accessibility: There are exactly 76 steep steps to reach the Hanuman Garhi temple. Pro-Tip: If you are traveling with elderly parents, local palanquins (dolis) are available at the base for a nominal fee.

Who Should Visit When?

Families & Senior Citizens

Feb-Mar or Oct-Nov: The weather is pleasant, and the walking distances between temples become much more manageable.

Budget Travellers

April, May, June, September: During the off-peak and summer/monsoon months, hotels and taxis drop their rates significantly.

Festival Seekers

March 26 (Ram Navami) & Nov 7 (Deepotsav): For those who want to witness the spiritual fervor of Ayodhya at its absolute peak.

Peace & Photography

July-August (Monsoon): Rains wash the dust away, the temples look vibrant, and the Saryu river is majestic.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best month to visit Ayodhya? expand_more
The best months to visit Ayodhya are from October to March during the winter season when temperatures are comfortable between 8°C and 25°C.
How hot is Ayodhya in summer? expand_more
In the summer months of April to June, temperatures in Ayodhya can soar up to 43°C, making afternoon sightseeing quite challenging.
When is Deepotsav in 2026? expand_more
Deepotsav in Ayodhya will be celebrated on November 7, 2026.
What time does the Saryu Aarti start? expand_more
The Saryu Aarti starts at 7:00 PM during the summer and 6:00 PM during the winter. It is highly recommended to arrive 30 minutes early to get a good spot.
What is the best time for Ram Mandir Darshan to avoid crowds? expand_more
To avoid peak crowds (which occur between 11 AM and 4 PM), visit the Ram Mandir either early morning between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM, or in the evening after 5:00 PM.
When should I book hotels for Deepotsav? expand_more
Hotels in Ayodhya book out completely 3 months before Deepotsav. For a November festival, you must secure your hotel booking by August.
Is it safe to visit Ayodhya during the monsoon? expand_more
Yes, but there is some risk of flooding near the riverbanks. The monsoon season (July-September) offers a peaceful atmosphere with lush green scenery along the Saryu River.
Are there palanquins at Hanuman Garhi? expand_more
Yes, there are 76 steps to reach Hanuman Garhi. Palanquins (dolis) are available for senior citizens or anyone unable to climb the stairs.
