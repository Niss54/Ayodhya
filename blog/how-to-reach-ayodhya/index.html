How to Reach Ayodhya 2026: By Flight, Train & Road — Complete Guide

calendar_today Updated: August 2026 schedule 10 Min Read flight_takeoff Travel Guide

Planning a pilgrimage to the holy city of Lord Ram in 2026? With the rapid development of infrastructure, reaching Ayodhya has never been easier. Whether you are flying into the newly built Maharishi Valmiki International Airport, taking a high-speed Vande Bharat train, or driving down the expressways, this comprehensive 2000-word guide breaks down every transport option available.

flight BY FLIGHT: Maharishi Valmiki International Airport

Maharishi Valmiki International Airport Ayodhya Dham (AYJ)

Inaugurated recently, this state-of-the-art airport is located just 10 km from the Ram Mandir, making it the most convenient entry point for domestic and international pilgrims.

  • airlines Operating Airlines: IndiGo, SpiceJet, and Air India.
  • route Direct Routes: Flights are currently available from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Chennai, Patna, Jaipur, and Darbhanga.
  • local_taxi Airport to Ram Mandir: Pre-paid taxi (₹300-₹500) or electric vehicle / e-rickshaw (₹100-₹200). Travel time is approximately 20-30 minutes depending on traffic.

Alternative Option: Lucknow Airport (LKO)

If direct flights to Ayodhya are fully booked or unavailable from your city, flying into Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport, Lucknow (LKO) is the best alternative. It is one of the busiest airports in North India with excellent connectivity.

  • Distance: 135 km from Ayodhya.
  • Transfer: A direct taxi ride via the NH-27 expressway takes exactly 2.5 hours.

train BY TRAIN: Ayodhya Dham Junction

The Indian Railways has massively upgraded Ayodhya's rail infrastructure. The city is served by two main railway stations:

Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY)

This is the primary and newly renovated station. It is located a mere 2.7 km from the Ram Mandir. This station is designed to handle millions of pilgrims with modern amenities.

Ayodhya Cantt (AYC)

Formerly known as Faizabad Junction, this is the secondary station located about 8 km from Ram Mandir. Many long-distance trains terminate here.

Key Trains & The Vande Bharat Express

The crown jewel of rail travel to Ayodhya is the Vande Bharat Express. If you are traveling from New Delhi, this semi-high-speed train offers a comfortable and rapid journey.

  • Departure: Anand Vihar Terminal, Delhi at 6:10 AM
  • Arrival: Ayodhya Cantt at 2:30 PM
  • Duration: 8 hours 20 minutes

Rail Distances from Major Cities

  • From Lucknow: 135 km
  • From Varanasi: 191 km
  • From Prayagraj: 157 km
  • From Gorakhpur: 164 km

directions_car BY ROAD: Highways & Buses

Ayodhya is seamlessly connected to major North Indian cities via national highways, making road trips highly enjoyable.

UPSRTC & Private Buses

The Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (UPSRTC) operates a 24-hour bus service to Ayodhya from major hubs like Lucknow, Gorakhpur, and Varanasi. If you are traveling from Delhi, several private operators offer luxurious overnight AC sleeper buses (journey time approx. 10-12 hours).

Self-Drive Routes

  • From Lucknow: Take the NH-27 (Lucknow - Gorakhpur Highway). It's a straight, 4-lane expressway ride of 135 km taking around 2.5 hours.
  • From Gorakhpur: Drive down NH-27/28 for about 135 km (2.5 hours).
  • From Delhi: Take the Yamuna Expressway to Agra, then the Agra-Lucknow Expressway, and finally NH-27 to Ayodhya. Total distance is ~636 km (10-11 hours).
