How to Reach Ayodhya from Delhi, Mumbai & Lucknow

For millions of devotees across the globe, the call to visit the birthplace of Lord Shri Ram is an irresistible spiritual yearning. Figuring out exactly how to reach Ayodhya smoothly is the very first step toward a fulfilling pilgrimage. With the recent inauguration of the grand Ram Mandir, the holy city of Ayodhya has seen a massive upgrade in its transportation infrastructure. The days of complicated transfers and long, arduous journeys are over. Today, you can fly, take a high-speed train, or drive down modern expressways straight into the heart of this divine destination.

Whether you are planning to travel from the bustling streets of Delhi, the coastal metropolis of Mumbai, or the neighboring city of Lucknow, reaching Ayodhya Dham is now incredibly convenient. A well-planned yatra allows you to arrive feeling fresh, focused, and ready for your sacred darshan. In this comprehensive travel guide crafted by our local Ayodhya travel experts, we will break down all the transportation options available. We will guide you through the best flights, train routes, and roadways so you can focus entirely on your devotion and peace of mind.

By Air — Maharishi Valmiki International Airport

One of the most remarkable additions to Ayodhya’s infrastructure is the newly built Maharishi Valmiki International Airport, Ayodhya Dham (Airport Code: AYJ). Reaching Ayodhya by air is now the fastest and most comfortable way for pilgrims arriving from distant corners of India and the world. Designed with a stunning architectural blend that echoes the grandeur of the Ram Mandir itself, stepping into this airport immediately fills your heart with a sense of reverence and anticipation.

Key Airport Details

  • Airport Code: AYJ
  • Distance to Ram Mandir: Approximately 8 km
  • Direct Ayodhya airport flights: Daily services from major metro cities including Delhi, Mumbai, and Ahmedabad.

If you are coming from Mumbai, the direct flight takes less than two and a half hours, transforming a previously tiring multi-day journey into a quick morning trip. Flights from Delhi take barely an hour and a half. Upon landing at the airport, you are merely 8 kilometers away from the sacred Ram Janmabhoomi. The short drive into the city takes only 20 to 30 minutes. To make this final leg of your journey seamless, you can easily pre-book an Ayodhya taxi service, ensuring our trusted driver is waiting for you right at the arrivals terminal.

By Train — Ayodhya Dham Junction

For generations, the rhythmic clatter of train wheels has been the soundtrack of the Indian pilgrimage. The Indian Railways has beautifully revamped the main railway station, now officially known as the Ayodhya Dham Junction train station. The moment you step off the train, you are greeted by an imposing, temple-like facade that sets the spiritual tone for your stay.

The biggest advantage of traveling by train is the proximity. Ayodhya Dham Junction is situated a mere 2 kilometers away from the Ram Mandir. You can practically feel the divine energy of the mandir bells echoing near the platforms. The station is exceptionally well-connected to major hubs across India. Premium trains like the Vande Bharat Express have drastically reduced travel times. For instance, the Vande Bharat from Delhi to Ayodhya takes around 8 hours, offering world-class comfort, catering, and large viewing windows.

Even if you are traveling from Kolkata, Mumbai, or Chennai, there are direct express and mail trains operating daily or weekly. Upon exiting the station, e-rickshaws and auto-rickshaws are readily available. However, if you are traveling with family and luggage, having a pre-arranged vehicle ensures you can head straight to your hotel to freshen up before heading out for aarti and darshan.

By Road — From Delhi, Lucknow & Varanasi

The road networks leading to Ayodhya have undergone a massive transformation, making road trips a highly favored option, especially for groups and families. The highways are smooth, well-lit, and dotted with convenient rest stops offering delicious local food.

Delhi to Ayodhya by Road

The distance from Delhi to Ayodhya is approximately 600 kilometers. By car or a comfortable Volvo bus, the journey generally takes around 10 to 12 hours. The route via the Yamuna Expressway and Agra-Lucknow Expressway is an absolute pleasure to drive on. Many pilgrims prefer driving down overnight, reaching Ayodhya early in the morning just in time for a holy dip in the Saryu River at Ram Ki Paidi before proceeding for darshan.

Lucknow to Ayodhya

Lucknow serves as a major gateway for those who cannot find direct flights or trains to Ayodhya. The distance is a short 130 kilometers. Thanks to the Purvanchal Expressway, this distance can be covered in a breezy 2.5 to 3 hours. If you opt for a Lucknow to Ayodhya taxi, the fare typically ranges from ₹2,500 to ₹3,000, depending on your choice of vehicle (sedan or SUV). This private cab option is incredibly popular for its sheer comfort. Alternatively, regular state transport (UPSRTC) buses and private Volvos operate from Lucknow’s Alambagh and Kaiserbagh bus stands, with tickets affordably priced between ₹200 and ₹400.

Varanasi to Ayodhya

Many pilgrims combine their Kashi Vishwanath darshan with Ram Janmabhoomi. The road trip from Varanasi is around 200 kilometers and takes about 4 to 5 hours. A dedicated taxi ride offers the most seamless transition between these two incredibly powerful spiritual hubs.

Local Transport Within Ayodhya

Once you have reached Ayodhya, navigating the city requires a bit of local know-how. The streets near the main temple complexes—especially around Ram Janmabhoomi, Hanuman Garhi, and Kanak Bhawan—can get quite crowded. Large four-wheelers are often restricted from entering the immediate vicinity of the main shrines to maintain pedestrian safety and spiritual decorum.

The most practical and eco-friendly way to move around locally is by using e-rickshaws. They are inexpensive, readily available everywhere, and can navigate the narrowest of lanes with ease. For participating in the Panchkoshi or Chaudah Koshi Parikrama, walking is the traditional and most spiritually rewarding method. However, for sightseeing across the broader Ayodhya and Faizabad area—including visits to Guptar Ghat or Surya Kund—hiring a dedicated local taxi for the day is highly recommended to save time and avoid the heat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the nearest airport to Ayodhya?

The nearest airport is the Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ) located directly in Ayodhya, just 8km away from the majestic Ram Mandir.

How far is Ayodhya Dham Junction from Ram Mandir?

Ayodhya Dham Junction is incredibly close, situated just 2km from the sacred Ram Janmabhoomi temple. It takes merely 10 to 15 minutes by auto or cab.

How much does a taxi cost from Lucknow to Ayodhya?

Are there direct flights from Delhi and Mumbai to Ayodhya?

Yes, direct flights operate daily from major cities including Delhi, Mumbai, and Ahmedabad to the newly inaugurated Ayodhya Airport.
