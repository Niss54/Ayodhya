Deepotsav Ayodhya 2026: Date, Celebration Guide & Tour Packages

Prepare for the most mesmerizing Diwali of your life. Witness 25+ lakh diyas illuminating the sacred banks of the Saryu River.

calendar_month Updated: August 2026 schedule 8 Min Read

Imagine the night sky ablaze with the golden glow of millions of earthen lamps, the sound of Vedic chants echoing through the air, and the entire city dressed in festive splendor. This is Deepotsav Ayodhya, the grandest celebration of Diwali in the world. If you are planning to attend Deepotsav 2026, this comprehensive guide covers everything from exact dates to the best tour packages.

1. What is Deepotsav? The Spiritual Significance

Deepotsav (Festival of Lights) is the modern recreation of the historical Diwali described in the Ramayana. It celebrates the triumphant return of Lord Ram, Goddess Sita, and Lord Lakshman to Ayodhya after 14 years of exile and their victory over Ravana. Today, the Uttar Pradesh government organizes this mega-event, turning the entire temple town into a glowing spectacle that attracts devotees and tourists from across the globe.

2. Deepotsav 2026 Date

Deepotsav is traditionally celebrated on Chhoti Diwali (the day before the main Diwali festival). The expected Deepotsav 2026 date is November 8, 2026 (Sunday). Mark your calendars, because this weekend will see Ayodhya transform into the spiritual capital of the world.

3. The World Record: 25+ Lakh Diyas

Every year, Ayodhya breaks its own Guinness World Record. For 2026, the administration and thousands of volunteers (mostly students from Awadh University) are preparing to light an unprecedented 25+ lakh (2.5 million) diyas along the 51 ghats of the Saryu River. The sheer scale of this lighting is a breathtaking sight that must be seen in person to be fully appreciated.

4. Key Events to Witness

Deepotsav is not just about the diyas; it is a multi-day cultural extravaganza. Key highlights include:

  • Maha Saryu Aarti: A massive, synchronized evening aarti performed by scores of priests at Naya Ghat.
  • Ram Leela Performances: International and national theatre troupes enact scenes from the Ramayana.
  • 3D Holographic Laser Show: A stunning modern laser mapping show projected across the river recounting the epic of Ramayana.
  • Grand Procession (Shobha Yatra): Elaborate floats depicting various chapters of Lord Ram's life parade through the city streets.

5. Best Viewing Spots

To get the best photographs and experience the magic, you need to know where to stand:

  • Ram Ki Paidi: The epicenter of the record-breaking diya lighting. Arrive by 3:00 PM to secure a standing spot.
  • Naya Ghat: The best location to witness the VIP Saryu Aarti and laser shows.
  • Janki Ghat & Guptar Ghat: Slightly less crowded but equally beautiful, offering serene views of the illuminated river.

6. Crucial Travel Tips for Deepotsav 2026

Attending a festival of this magnitude requires careful planning. Here is what you must know:

  • Advance Hotel Booking: Hotels in Ayodhya sell out 4-5 months before Diwali. Book immediately if you plan to visit in November.
  • Traffic & Vehicle Zones: Commercial and private four-wheelers are banned from entering the main city (Dharam Path/Ram Path) during Deepotsav. You will have to park in designated lots 5-8 kilometers away and rely on government electric shuttle services or e-rickshaws.
  • Security Checks: Carry your original Aadhaar card at all times. Expect massive crowds and tight police cordons.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the date for Deepotsav Ayodhya 2026?

Deepotsav Ayodhya 2026 will be celebrated on November 8, 2026 (Sunday), the eve of Diwali.

How many diyas will be lit in Ayodhya Deepotsav 2026?

The Uttar Pradesh government aims to set a new world record by lighting over 25 lakh diyas along the banks of the Saryu River.

Where is the best place to watch Deepotsav in Ayodhya?

Ram Ki Paidi and Naya Ghat are the best locations to witness the grand lighting of the diyas, laser shows, and the Saryu Aarti.

Do I need a ticket to attend Deepotsav?

Entry to general viewing areas is free, but VIP seating and certain ghats may require special passes issued by the administration.

How far in advance should I book my hotel?

Because Deepotsav attracts millions of global tourists, you should book your Ayodhya hotel at least 3-4 months in advance.

Can I bring my vehicle near Ram Ki Paidi during Deepotsav?

No, strict traffic restrictions are enforced. Heavy vehicles are banned, and you will need to park in designated zones outside the main city center and use shuttle services.
