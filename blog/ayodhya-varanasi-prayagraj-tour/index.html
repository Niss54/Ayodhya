Ayodhya Varanasi Prayagraj Tour 2026: Complete 5-Day Itinerary & Cost Guide

Embark on India's most sacred pilgrimage. A comprehensive breakdown of the holy UP circuit, featuring the best route, detailed itineraries, and exact cost planning.

calendar_month Updated: August 2026 schedule 10 Min Read

The cultural and spiritual heart of India beats in Uttar Pradesh. Combining the ancient wisdom of Varanasi, the sacred confluence at Prayagraj, and the divine birthland of Ayodhya creates a pilgrimage that purifies the soul. If you are planning the Ayodhya Varanasi Prayagraj tour 2026, this guide will provide you with a meticulously planned 5-day itinerary and realistic cost estimations.

1. India's Holiest Circuit: The Dharma-Mukti-Sangam Symbolism

This three-city circuit is not just a tour; it is a journey through the core concepts of Hinduism:

  • Varanasi (Mukti): The city of Lord Shiva, believed to grant liberation (Moksha) from the cycle of rebirth.
  • Prayagraj (Sangam): The holy Triveni Sangam, where the Ganga, Yamuna, and mythical Saraswati rivers meet, washing away earthly sins.
  • Ayodhya (Dharma): The birthplace of Lord Ram, representing righteousness, duty, and perfect governance (Ram Rajya).

2. Choosing Your Route: Two Great Options

When planning your trip, you have two primary directional routes to choose from. Both are connected by excellent, newly constructed expressways.

Option 1: Ayodhya to Varanasi

Arrive in Ayodhya, seek Lord Ram's blessings, travel down to Prayagraj for the holy dip, and culminate your journey with the powerful Ganga Aarti in Varanasi. Best if you are flying into Maharishi Valmiki Airport.

Option 2: Varanasi to Ayodhya (Recommended)

Arrive in Varanasi (Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport), experience Kashi, move to Prayagraj, and end your spiritual journey at the grand Ram Mandir in Ayodhya. This flow perfectly balances the intense energy of Kashi with the serene devotion of Ayodhya.

3. Recommended Sequence: The 5-Day Master Itinerary

We highly recommend the Varanasi (Day 1-2) → Prayagraj (Day 3) → Ayodhya (Day 4-5) route. Here is the day-by-day breakdown of this perfect 5-day schedule.

Day 1: Arrival in Varanasi & Evening Ganga Aarti

Arrive in Varanasi. Check into your hotel and rest. In the late afternoon, proceed to the Kashi Vishwanath Temple corridor for a divine darshan. As the sun sets, secure a spot (or a boat) at Dashashwamedh Ghat to witness the world-famous, spellbinding Ganga Aarti.

Day 2: Morning Boat Ride, Ghats & Sarnath

Wake up before dawn for a serene subah-e-Banaras boat ride along the ghats. Witness the mesmerizing sunrise. After breakfast, drive 10 km to Sarnath, the place where Lord Buddha gave his first sermon. Explore the Dhamek Stupa and museum.

Day 3: The Holy Dip at Prayagraj

Check out and drive to Prayagraj (approx. 2.5 hours). Head straight to the Triveni Sangam. Take a boat to the exact confluence point for a holy dip. Visit the famous Bade Hanuman Ji Mandir (the only sleeping Hanuman idol) and Anand Bhawan. Overnight stay in Prayagraj.

Day 4: Arrival in Ayodhya & Ram Mandir Darshan

Drive to Ayodhya via the scenic highway (approx. 3.5 hours). Check into your Ayodhya hotel. In the afternoon, proceed for the much-awaited darshan of Ram Lalla at the grand Ram Mandir. Later, climb the steps of Hanuman Garhi for the blessings of Bajrangbali.

Day 5: Kanak Bhawan & Departure

Start your final day visiting the beautiful Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal. Spend a quiet hour at Ram Ki Paidi. Depending on your flight or train schedule, our cab will drop you at the Ayodhya Airport/Station for your journey back home.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the best time to do the Ayodhya Varanasi Prayagraj tour?

The best time is between October and March when the weather is pleasant. Avoid peak summer (May-June) due to extreme heat.

How many days are needed for this UP circuit?

A minimum of 5 days is recommended to comfortably cover Varanasi (2 days), Prayagraj (1 day), and Ayodhya (2 days).

Is it better to start the trip from Varanasi or Ayodhya?

Both routes are feasible. Starting from Varanasi and ending in Ayodhya is popular because Varanasi has excellent flight connectivity and it allows you to culminate your spiritual journey at the Ram Mandir.

What is the budget for the 5-day tour?

A budget trip starts around ₹8,000 per person, a mid-range comfortable trip is about ₹14,000, and a premium luxury experience costs ₹22,000+ per person.

Is the Triveni Sangam boat ride included in tour packages?

Standard tour packages include transportation to the Sangam, but the specific boat ride fee is usually paid directly by the traveler based on negotiation, unless specifically booked as an inclusive premium package.

Can elderly people comfortably complete this 3-city tour?

Yes, by booking a private AC cab and pacing the 5-day itinerary properly, elderly pilgrims can easily complete the yatra without extreme physical strain.

What is the distance between Varanasi, Prayagraj, and Ayodhya?

Varanasi to Prayagraj is about 120 km (2.5 hours). Prayagraj to Ayodhya is about 160 km (3.5 hours).
