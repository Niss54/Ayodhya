Kartik Purnima Ayodhya 2026: Date, Saryu Snan, Deep Daan & Travel Guide

calendar_today Published: August 2026 schedule 8 Min Read celebration Festival Guide

Ayodhya is synonymous with Diwali and Ram Navami, but there is another deeply significant festival that transforms the spiritual landscape of Ram Nagari: Kartik Purnima. Known for massive gatherings by the Saryu River and the mesmerizing lighting of lamps (Deep Daan), this festival is an unmissable spiritual experience. Here is your complete 2026 guide to celebrating Kartik Purnima in Ayodhya.

1. The Significance of Kartik Purnima

Kartik Purnima falls on the full moon day of the highly auspicious Hindu month of Kartik. The religious significance of this day spans across multiple traditions:

  • Vishnu & Shiva Significance: It is believed that Lord Vishnu incarnated as Matsya (the fish avatar) on this day. It is also celebrated as Dev Diwali in Kashi to mark Lord Shiva's victory over the demon Tripurasura.
  • Tulsi Vivah Conclusion: It marks the joyous conclusion of the Tulsi Vivah ceremonies that begin on Prabodhini Ekadashi.
  • Guru Nanak Jayanti: Coinciding beautifully with this date is the birth anniversary of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, adding a pan-Indian spiritual harmony to the day.

2. Why Ayodhya is Special on Kartik Purnima

While Kashi is famous for Dev Diwali, Ayodhya holds a unique charm on Kartik Purnima.

water

The Holy Saryu Snan

On this specific day, bathing in the Saryu River is considered as spiritually purifying as a dip in the Ganga. Devotees start crowding the ghats from midnight just to take the holy plunge at dawn.

local_fire_department

The Magic of Deep Daan

As the sun sets, thousands of earthen lamps (diyas) are floated into the river. The reflections at Ram Ki Paidi create an atmosphere that beautifully mirrors the grandeur of Deepotsav.

temple_hindu

Ram Mandir Special Darshan

The new Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir conducts a highly elaborate Shringar (adornment) and special evening Aarti. The temple complex is decorated with flowers and traditional rangolis.

3. The Perfect Timing Guide

To avoid massive crowds and experience the festival peacefully, follow this timing chart:

  • alarm
    4:00 AM – 6:00 AM: Saryu Snan (Holy Bath). Arrive early to beat the massive influx of rural devotees that arrive by sunrise.
  • sunny_snowing
    6:00 AM – 9:00 AM: Morning Darshan at Ram Mandir. Secure your entry in the first slot.
  • wb_twilight
    5:30 PM: Deep Daan at Ram Ki Paidi Ghats. Watch the sunset and the lighting of the lamps.
  • celebration
    6:00 PM: The Grand Saryu Aarti. Arrive at least 30 minutes early to grab a good viewing spot on the steps.

map Ultimate Trip Idea: The 18-Day Stay

In 2026, Deepotsav falls on November 7, and Kartik Purnima is on November 24. For the ultimate spiritual retreat, many pilgrims plan an extended 18-day stay in Ayodhya, soaking in the continuous festive atmosphere of the entire Kartik month, visiting nearby teerths like Naimisharanya and Chitrakoot in between.

4. Practical Travel Tips for 2026

  • Hotel Bookings: While not as intensely packed as Deepotsav, Kartik Purnima still draws lakhs of people. Hotels are easier to book, but securing a room 3-4 weeks in advance is highly recommended.
  • Weather Advisory: Late November in Ayodhya brings chilly mornings and cool evenings. If you plan for a 4:00 AM Saryu bath, carrying a thick towel, warm shawl, and light jacket is essential.
  • Transportation: E-rickshaws will be blocked from entering the main Ram Path corridor on the actual festival day. Be prepared for a 1-2 km walk.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Kartik Purnima in 2026? expand_more
Kartik Purnima falls on Tuesday, November 24, 2026.
What is the best time for Saryu Snan on Kartik Purnima? expand_more
The most auspicious and peaceful time for Saryu Snan is between 4:00 AM and 6:00 AM, before the heaviest crowds arrive.
What is Deep Daan? expand_more
Deep Daan is the ritual of floating lighted earthen lamps (diyas) in a sacred river. In Ayodhya, it takes place at dusk (around 5:30 PM) along the Ram Ki Paidi ghats.
Is it difficult to get a hotel during Kartik Purnima? expand_more
While very busy, getting a hotel during Kartik Purnima is slightly easier than during Deepotsav. However, booking 3-4 weeks in advance is highly recommended.
What is the weather like in Ayodhya during November? expand_more
The weather is very pleasant and cool. Temperatures range from 15°C to 25°C. It is advisable to carry a light jacket or warm shawl for early morning snan.
